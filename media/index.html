Selected press coverage and media mentions on research papers.

Can Virtual Reality Bring Climate Change Closer to Home? Bay Area Researchers Think So

KQED Science · December 11, 2025 · By Laura Klivans

A Stanford lab shows that when people use virtual reality to explore an unfamiliar place, it deepens their emotional connection to, and concern about, climate change impacts far from home.

Researchers Take New 'Mixed Reality' Headsets for a Spin

Stanford News · February 1, 2024 · By Adam Hadhazy

Stanford researchers tested mixed reality headsets that blend views of the real world with digital content, finding both moments of awe and unsettlement, and urging caution regarding prolonged use.

Virtual Reality Could Help Those Not Taking Climate Change Seriously

MSN News · 2025

Coverage of Stanford research showing how virtual reality experiences can shift attitudes toward climate change among skeptical audiences.

Virtual Reality Helps People Understand and Care About Distant Communities

EurekAlert! / Stanford University · October 24, 2025

Stanford-led research shows immersive VR can make distant places feel more immediate and climate-related impacts personally relevant, with findings published in Scientific Reports.

A New Study Analyzes Collaborative Behaviors During Task Building Within Social Virtual Reality

UF College of Journalism and Communications · May 4, 2026 · By Sue Wagner

Coverage of research on how immersive collaborative behaviors unfold over time in VR, and how they relate to group closeness and collaborative outcomes, published in Cognitive Science.

Five Things to Do in Virtual Reality — and Five to Avoid

ScienceDaily / Stanford University · May 30, 2025

Coverage of a Nature Human Behaviour review of 30 years of VR research, identifying when VR works best and why it has struggled with mainstream consumer adoption.
