Research Themes

Technology and Group Behavior (TGB)

I study how technology shapes group communication, collaboration, and decision-making to improve productivity and satisfaction.

Human-Centric AI, Social Media & Immersive Media (HCAI)

I design and investigate the role of AI, social media, and VR for invoking prosocial behavior change through qualitative and quantitative methods.

Healthcare Operations, Policy and Economic Analysis (HCP)

I develop methodological approaches and investigate how health and technology policy influence youth mental health and economic outcomes.
