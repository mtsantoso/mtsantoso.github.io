Monique Santoso

I am a Media Psychology PhD candidate at Stanford University’s Department of Communication, with a PhD minor in Management Science and Engineering, supported by the Knight-Hennessy Scholarship, the EDGE Fellowship, the Emerging Environmental Scholars Research Fellowship, and the PCJ in the Bay Fellowship. I am advised by Prof. Jeremy Bailenson at the Virtual Human Interaction Lab.

My work draws on human perception and cognition to build theoretical and empirical frameworks for designing and evaluating personalized interventions in real-world settings.

Previously, I obtained my BA in Cognitive Psychology and Environmental Science at Middlebury College and conducted research at Boston Children’s Hospital and Harvard University, investigating the impact of technology and health policy on youth wellbeing and socioeconomic outcomes.
