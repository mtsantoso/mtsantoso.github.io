I am a Media Psychology PhD candidate at Stanford University's [Department of Communication](https://comm.stanford.edu/), with a PhD minor in [Management Science and Engineering](https://msande.stanford.edu/), supported by the [Knight-Hennessy Scholarship](https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/), the [EDGE Fellowship](https://vpge.stanford.edu/fellowships-funding/vpge-fellowships/edge-doctoral-fellowship-program), the [Emerging Environmental Scholars Research Fellowship](https://woods.stanford.edu/research/funding-opportunities/emerging-environment-scholars-research-awards), and the [PCJ in the Bay Fellowship](https://haas.stanford.edu/resources/community-partners/partnerships-climate-justice-bay-area). I am advised by [Prof. Jeremy Bailenson](https://profiles.stanford.edu/jeremy-bailenson) at the [Virtual Human Interaction Lab](https://vhil.stanford.edu/).

My work draws on human perception and cognition to build theoretical and empirical frameworks for designing and evaluating personalized interventions in real-world settings. 

Previously, I obtained my BA in Cognitive Psychology and Environmental Science at [Middlebury College](https://www.middlebury.edu/college/academics/environmental-studies) and conducted research at Boston Children's Hospital and Harvard University, investigating the impact of technology and health policy on youth wellbeing and socioeconomic outcomes.
